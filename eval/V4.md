# v4 — automatic light (index.html?v4, in progress)

Goal: open a DNG and get a Gold 200 print with no light sliders. Stages (CLAUDE.md):
scene (DNG) → film conversion → film + grain → lab → scanner look (later: tab Scanner, Frontier | none | Noritsu) → output.

## Film conversion (web/lib/conversion.js)
- Scene EV100 from the DNG/ORF shot data: log2(N²/t) − log2(ISO/100) − BaselineExposure (the phone held back that much).
- The camera that "took" the frame is a single-use camera (user choice): f/10, 1/125 s, Gold 200 at box speed.
  Film exposure offset = scene EV100 − camera EV100 (12.6), clamped −6…+5. Indoors the negative is thin, in the sun dense.
- Indigo DNGs (iPhone 17 Pro Max): sun 15–15.7 → +2.4…+3.1 EV; cat room 11.5 → −1.1; dark room 8.5 → −4.2.
  A compact camera (f/3.5, 1/30) would have exposed all of them correctly: the indoor look comes from the camera choice.
- JPEGs and raws without shot data: offset 0 (exposed as metered).
- The engine applies it as `camera.exposure_compensation_ev`; with `enlarger.print_exposure_compensation` (default on)
  the print exposure is normalised for it, so the print keeps its brightness and the negative's density changes
  (sf-eval, cat DNG: L* 40.6 / 40.1 / 41.7 at 0 / −1.1 / −2.5 EV).
- The metered exposure (engine auto exposure on the thumb, as v3 Auto) is kept and the v3 highlight dodging (Luci auto)
  stays on at contrast 0.

## Paper print (no scanner)
`io.scan_film = false`, `scanner.model = null`: the negative is printed on Kodak Portra Endura (common.js PAPER) through
the enlarger (TH-KG3, neutral filters Y 55 / M 65). Unfiltered, the print is neutral in daylight (sea DNG mids
a*/b* +2.3/+3.8) and carries the room's light indoors (cat DNG +11.5/+13.5).

## Lab (web/lib/lab.js)
The printer's colour analyser: enlarger Y / M shifts that remove LAB_K = 0.7 of the cast, measured on midtone prints of
the metered thumb (opponent log chroma, saturated colours down-weighted), solved from two probe prints (filters act
linearly: +1 Y → b* −1.2; +1 M → a* −0.9, b* +0.6). Cat DNG: Y 13.9 / M 10.0 → mids +1.4/+4.3; sea DNG: Y −0.5 / M −1.4.
Temperatura is not seen by the lab (the slider is the user's on top). Colour keys: Magenta/Verde, Giallo/Blu move the
enlarger filters on top of the lab's, half a unit per step.

## Engine calibration inputs
Camera EV, enlarger filters and scan_film / scanner model are baked when the engine is built (spektrafilm-wasm
`Engine.update` ignores them): `syncEngine` in app.js rebuilds the engine when they change (v3: never, key `{}`).
The lab costs four builds at load (~4 s on SwiftShader).

## Findings so far
- Teal shadows do not come out of film + paper by themselves (cat at −2.5 EV: deep shadows +1.8/+1.5): in v3 they were
  the Frontier model's toe parameter. Open question for the user.
- `scripts/eval/render.cjs` does not render ?v4 correctly (its output matched the pre-calibration build); v4 renders
  are checked with a playwright script that loads the photo through #pick.
