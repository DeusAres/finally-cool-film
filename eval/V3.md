# v3 — automatic highlights (default app; index.html?v2 = the previous v2 app)

Goal: only Esposizione + Contrasto (+ Contrasto luci); highlights handled automatically, keeping cloud modelling and detail.

## Why a curve alone cannot do it
A per-pixel tone curve that keeps a bright cloud's internal contrast must flatten every tone at that level;
with more contrast the scanner shoulder starts earlier and clouds go flat/white (or, with the v2 highlight lock,
pale/hue-shifted). Minilabs solve it spatially: Fuji's digital dodging (patents US 6,987,589 / US 6,700,685 /
US 6,807,316: low-frequency density compressed, detail untouched; Frontier "Hyper Tone" is reportedly this,
second-hand), the darkroom's burning-in of skies.

## Model (web/lib/dodge.js, INPUT_WGSL_V3 in web/lib/lens-gpu.js)
- Base B: self-guided filter of log2 luma of the AutoSetup thumbnail (256 px long side), radius 8 % of the
  long side, eps 1.0 (EV²). Frame-referenced, so preview, export tiles and any resolution share it.
- Shift in the input pass, scene light before the exposure gain: `s = A (1 - exp(-rho e / A))`,
  `e = max(b - knee, 0)`, `b = max(B(pos), own log2 luma)`. Slope <= rho = 0.8 < 1: no tone reversal (no halo or
  rim at skylines: a plain ramp gave a bright rim, measured), constant shift inside bright areas (modelling kept).
- knee = median(B) + 1.0 EV (sweep, 8 DNGs: same highlight gain as +0.5, mid contrast kept at 0.96 instead of 0.86 at Contrasto +1); A solved so the 99th percentile of B lands at median + head(contrast),
  head = 2.6 - 1.2 c (c >= 0), 2.6 - 1.5 c (c < 0): more contrast, earlier shoulder, more dodging.
- AutoSetup and the exposure meter still see the undodged thumbnail (dodging them too made AutoSetup
  brighten the whole frame back, measured).
- Contrast: `frontier.debug_contrast_mode = 2` (rational density remap about mid grey, ends fixed; no lock).

## Ombre (local shadow lift) and speculars (v3.7)
- Ombre slider 0..1: areas whose wide base is below median + 1.5 EV get up to 1.5 EV,
  `S (1 - exp(-0.8 e / S))`; bright areas (sky) never move. A dark pixel next to a bright area may lower its base
  by at most 1.5 EV (no dark rim at skylines).
- Highlight dodge: a bright pixel may raise its base by at most 1.5 EV, so small speculars / sun reflections on dark
  rock are not dodged.
- Rocks DNG (IDG_20261011_134131_210), Contrasto +0.5, browser: sky 142/169/203 unchanged, rocks mean luma 43 -> 78,
  sunlit tops p99 142 -> 189 at Ombre 1. Raising Esposizione instead burns the sky (the dodge is capped to stay
  halo-free): Ombre is the tool to expose both.

## v3.8: regression fix (rocks DNG: dark crushed rock, dull lit faces)
- Contrast curve mode 3 = v2 locked curve below mid grey (shadow toe kept) + free curve above. Mode 2 put 15 % of the
  rock under luma 15 at Contrasto +0.77; mode 3: 0 %.
- The pixel's own level only raises the dodge near / inside large bright areas (`near` map: base above the knee,
  dilated 3 thumbnail px). With the median on a dark subject, the lit rock faces were above the knee and got dodged
  (rock p95 196 -> 160); now 192 (no dodge: 196), same detail, sky unchanged, no skyline rim.
- Esposizione moved away from Auto shifts the dodge target by the same EV.

## Limits / next
- In scene light: halation and grain see the dodged light (a real Hyper Tone acts after the film).
- CPU fallback (no WebGPU) has no dodging.

## v3.17 Recupero luci locale (dodge.js `dodge()` after `dodge0()`)
Esposizione above Auto and Contrasto push bright areas up the scanner shoulder (sunlit plaster wall at +0.6 EV / contrast 0.15: texture
ratio 0.29, measured as median |highpass sigma 1.5| of log luma over the input's). The push beyond REC_FREE (evOff + 1.4 contrast - 0.25 EV)
is partly taken back (REC_RHO 0.5) from a fine edge-aware base (24-tap bilateral, 1.2 % of the long side, per frame), not from the pixel's
level, and tones below the base are opened (REC_DETAIL 1.0). Wall: user settings 0.29 -> 0.67 at -0.52 EV from white, contrast 0.5 0.31 -> 0.73;
no effect (bit-identical) at Auto exposure and contrast <= 0.18. CPU fallback `dodgeCPU` mirrors it (taps clamp at the region edge).
